# Legal information required before publishing final policies

Fill these placeholders with adult-operator details, then have an Indian technology/privacy lawyer review before treating policies as final.

| Placeholder | Purpose | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `[LEGAL OWNER NAME]` | Natural person or authorised signatory | Required |
| `[BUSINESS OR ENTITY NAME]` | Trading name / entity if any | Required |
| `[CONTACT ADDRESS]` | Correspondence address in India | Required |
| `[PRIVACY EMAIL]` | Privacy contact (currently operational: recallplus.website@gmail.com) | Confirm |
| `[SUPPORT EMAIL]` | Support contact (currently operational: recallplus.website@gmail.com) | Confirm |
| `[GRIEVANCE CONTACT]` | Grievance officer / channel under applicable law | Required |
| `[GOVERNING JURISDICTION]` | e.g. courts in [City], India | Required |
| `[EFFECTIVE DATE]` | Policy effective date | Required |
| `[POLICY VERSION]` | Monotonic policy version string | Required |

## Do not publish incomplete placeholder pages to production footers

Drafts live under `docs/legal/`. Production Privacy/Terms pages must remain accurate to the live product and must not invent registration numbers, lawyer names, or fake addresses.
