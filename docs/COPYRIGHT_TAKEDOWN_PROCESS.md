# Copyright takedown process (draft)

Contact: `recallplus.website@gmail.com` with subject `Copyright complaint`.

Please include:

1. Your contact details
2. Description of the work
3. URL or in-app location of the allegedly infringing material
4. Statement of good-faith belief and authority to act
5. Signature (physical or electronic)

On valid notice, Recall+ will review promptly, remove or restrict access where appropriate, and document the outcome.

This process is an operational draft and must be reviewed by counsel before being relied on as a formal statutory notice procedure.
